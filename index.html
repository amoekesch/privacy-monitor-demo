Announcements, rulemaking notices and enforcement actions

Announcements

October 29, 2026

Office adopts amended Rule 12-3.08 on retention of consumer rights request records

The Office of Consumer Data Protection today filed the amended Consumer Data Protection Rules, 7 ACR 12-3, with the Secretary of State following the October 22 public hearing. Under amended Rule 12-3.08, controllers must retain records of all consumer rights requests and the controller's response to each request for at least 36 months. This amendment supersedes the prior 24-month requirement and takes effect January 1, 2027. Controllers must also make these records available to the Office within 10 business days of a written demand.

September 18, 2026

Office opens public comment period on proposed amendments to 7 ACR 12-3

The Office invites written comment on proposed amendments to the Consumer Data Protection Rules, including record retention, universal opt-out mechanism recognition, and data broker registration. Comments are due October 16, 2026. A public hearing will be held October 22, 2026.

August 27, 2026

Office publishes fiscal year 2025 enforcement report

The report summarizes 41 investigations opened, 9 resolved by settlement, and total administrative penalties of $4.2 million. Common findings included missing notice at collection, failure to honor opt-out preference signals, and vendor contracts lacking required terms.

July 30, 2026

Office schedules stakeholder sessions on universal opt-out mechanisms

Three virtual sessions will be held in August to gather input on technical specifications for recognizing universal opt-out preference signals under Rule 12-3.14. Registration is encouraged but not required.

June 11, 2026

Office adopts 2027 data broker registration fee schedule

The annual registration fee for data brokers operating in Arcadia is set at $450 for the 2027 registration year. Registration opens January 2, 2027 and closes January 31, 2027.
